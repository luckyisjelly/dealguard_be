# Problem And USP

Secondhand transactions often fail because important conditions are scattered across product descriptions and chat messages. Price, refund rules, delivery fees, defects, included items, and trade place can change during negotiation, but buyers and sellers rarely keep a structured record.

DealGuard focuses on prevention and evidence preparation. It does not replace a marketplace. It records and verifies transaction conditions from chat, then turns them into a structured evidence package.

## Core USP

Existing secondhand platforms connect transactions. DealGuard records, verifies, and evidence-packages transaction conditions from chat.

## MVP Positioning

- Use manual product post and chat input.
- Do not crawl third-party platforms.
- Do not scrape private data.
- Use rule dictionaries and mock AI first.
- Keep the architecture ready for real AI and frontend integration.
